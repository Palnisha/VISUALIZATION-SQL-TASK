Summary of Findings: Customer Retention & Sales Analysis for Alt Mobility

1. Key Insights from SQL Queries
Our analysis of customer_orders.csv and payments.csv uncovered important trends in order fulfillment, sales performance, and payment success rates:
- Order Fulfillment Trends:- A significant portion of orders remain in-progress for extended periods, potentially indicating delays in processing or logistical challenges.
- Completed orders show stable growth month-over-month, suggesting improving operational efficiency.

- Revenue Insights:- High-value orders come predominantly from repeat customers, reinforcing the importance of retention strategies.
- Certain periods exhibit payment failures, potentially affecting revenue streams.

- Customer Segmentation & Repeat Ordering Behavior:- A majority of customers place repeat orders, indicating strong brand loyalty.
- New customers contribute a lower share of total revenue, highlighting an opportunity for improved customer onboarding strategies.

- Payment Success vs. Failure Trends:- A noticeable percentage of payments fail due to transaction errors, possibly linked to specific payment methods or customer demographics.
- Prepaid transactions have higher success rates than postpaid billing, hinting at reliability in upfront payments.



2. Observations from Customer Retention Analysis
Visualizing customer retention trends using Power BI heatmaps, line charts, and bar graphs yielded the following insights:
- Early Retention Trends:- Customers tend to repurchase within the first 3 months of their initial purchase.
- Retention gradually declines beyond Month 6, suggesting the need for targeted re-engagement efforts.

- Retention Across Cohorts:- Customers who made their first purchase in peak business months (e.g., festive periods) show stronger retention rates.
- Retention drops in off-season months, indicating a seasonal impact on repeat business.

- Retention Discrepancies in Payment Models:- Customers opting for subscription-based financing show higher retention than those purchasing via one-time payments.
- Retention among failed-payment users is significantly lower, underscoring the importance of payment reliability.



3. Recommendations for Alt Mobility
Based on these findings, here are key strategic recommendations:
- Optimize Order Fulfillment:- Reduce order delays by enhancing fleet logistics tracking and improving estimated delivery times.
- Introduce automated notifications for pending orders to keep customers engaged.

- Improve Customer Retention Strategies:- Implement a loyalty program rewarding frequent customers.
- Offer personalized renewal incentives for customers nearing 6-month usage decline periods.

- Enhance Payment Reliability:- Provide alternative payment methods with higher success rates.
- Strengthen follow-ups on failed transactions to minimize customer churn due to payment issues.
